## Description

Wahrly is a daily life assistant — say it once, see it on Home, close the day at night.

**Free**
• Tasks with a week rail and confirm-before-done
• Important Dates with gentle nudges
• On-device chat that creates and moves tasks
• Home timeline + Settings

**Wahrly Pro**
• Bills and Shopping
• Life, News, Invest
• Plan day (auto free gaps)
• Morning brief, Evening Clear, weekly brief
• Reminders, Home Screen widget
• Cloud AI chat and voice

Google Calendar is read-only when you connect it. Wahrly never opens your email.

Designed for a calm day: add what matters, see what’s next, finish the evening.

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Contact: rr.aliev96@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Wahrly (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Wahrly mobile application and related API. By using Wahrly, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service or connect your Google account.

1. Who we are

Wahrly is a personal AI life assistant that helps you organize tasks, reminders, and a calm daily plan. The product is operated by the Wahrly developer (contact below).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Wahrly, we may process:

  • Account information — email address and name you provide when signing in (including via Supabase Auth when configured).
  • App content you create — tasks, checklists, reminders, chat messages with Wahrly, and settings (stored on your device and/or in your Supabase project when connected).
  • Google account data (only after you tap “Connect with Google” and grant permission) — limited Calendar access described in section 3.
  • Technical data — basic server logs (timestamps, error messages) needed to keep the service running. We do not sell advertising profiles.

3. Google Calendar access

If you connect Google, Wahrly requests only calendar.readonly (plus basic account email for the connected-account label). Wahrly does not request Gmail scopes and does not read your mail. Reconnect in Settings if you connected earlier with mail scopes so Google drops them.

With those permissions, Wahrly may:

  • Read today’s events from your primary Google Calendar to show them on the Home signal timeline and to avoid those times when packing Plan day.

Wahrly does not:

  • Read, send, draft, delete, or modify your email.
  • Create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.
  • Read your calendar without your explicit Connect / Allow step.
  • Sell your Google content to third parties.

Google OAuth tokens (access / refresh) are stored on our API server so calendar events can refresh without asking you to Allow every day. You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings; we then delete the stored tokens for your account.

Wahrly’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and improve Wahrly features (tasks, chat, morning brief, calendar).
  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • Call AI providers (for example Groq or OpenAI) with the text needed to answer your chat — not for unrelated advertising.
  • Comply with law if required.

5. AI processing

When you chat with Wahrly, message text may be sent to our API server and then to the configured AI provider to generate a reply. Do not paste secrets (passwords, bank codes) into chat.

6. Storage and retention

Local app data stays on your device until you clear app storage or delete the account data. Server-side Google tokens are kept while your Google connection is active and removed on disconnect. Logs are kept only as long as needed for operations and abuse prevention.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with processors needed to run Wahrly (hosting, auth, AI APIs) under their terms, or when required by law.

8. Security

We use HTTPS and industry-standard practices to protect tokens and accounts. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please use a strong password for your Wahrly account.

9. Children’s privacy

Wahrly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account (clears local data too).
  • Delete local data by clearing app storage / uninstalling.
  • Email us if you need extra server-side cleanup after delete.
